What I study

Three connected lines of work, using longitudinal and multivariate methods in R, Mplus, Python, Amos, and SPSS.

Cyberbullying and adolescent well-being

How aggression, self-esteem, and peer relationships shape life satisfaction in Korean adolescents, including within-person change across the middle-to-high school transition.

Moderated mediationLongitudinal panelsLatent class analysis

Measurement and validation

Validating scales for counseling ethics, personality, mental contamination, and group leader self-efficacy.

Supervision and counselor wellness

Supervising international students in rural Appalachia, competency-based mentorship, and faculty wellness.
